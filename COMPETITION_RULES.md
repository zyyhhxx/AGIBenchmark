## 2. COMPETITION-SPECIFIC RULES

### 2.1 TEAM LIMITS
- Maximum Team size: **5**
- Team mergers allowed by Team leader
- For Hackathons, each team is allowed **one (1) Submission**; any Submissions submitted by Participants before merging into a Team will be unsubmitted.

### 2.2 SUBMISSION LIMITS
- **For Hackathons, each Team may submit one (1) Submission only.**

### 2.3 COMPETITION TIMELINE
- Dates on the competition's Overview > Timeline page.

### 2.4 COMPETITION DATA
- **None.** Competition Data will not be provided by Competition Sponsor.
- Upon joining, your Kaggle account is provisioned with extra quota ($50/day, $500/month) to run AI models for your benchmark.
- If you run out, email kaggle-benchmarks-agi-hackathon@google.com

### 2.5 WINNER LICENSE
- Winning Submission licensed under **CC0** (Open Source Initiative-approved, no commercial use limits)
- For generally commercially available software not owned by you, no license grant needed if Sponsor can procure it without undue expense
- Input data or pretrained models with incompatible licenses: no open source license required for those

### 2.6 EXTERNAL DATA AND TOOLS
- External Data allowed if publicly available and equally accessible to all Participants at no cost, or meets Reasonableness Standard
- AMLT (Automated Machine Learning Tools) allowed with appropriate license

### 2.7 ELIGIBILITY
- Employees/interns/contractors of Competition Entities may enter but **not win Prizes**
- Competition Entities = Google LLC, Kaggle Inc., and affiliates

### 2.8 WINNER'S OBLIGATIONS
- Deliver final model's software code and documentation
- For Hackathons, deliverables as described on Competition Website (may not be software code)
- Grant CC0 license
- Sign and return Prize acceptance documents, tax forms

### 2.9 GOVERNING LAW
- California law, Federal or State courts of Santa Clara County, California

## 3. GENERAL COMPETITION RULES

### 3.1 ELIGIBILITY
- Registered Kaggle account
- 18+ or age of majority
- Not resident of Crimea, DNR, LNR, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea
- Not under U.S. export controls/sanctions

### 3.4 COMPETITION ENTRY
- NO PURCHASE NECESSARY
- Upon joining, extra quota provisioned ($50/day, $500/month)
- By making a submission, you declare everything submitted is true to your knowledge

### 3.5 INDIVIDUALS AND TEAMS
- One unique Kaggle account per person
- One Team per person
- **No private sharing outside of Teams**
- Public code sharing permitted on Kaggle forums/notebooks

### 3.7 DETERMINING WINNERS
- Hackathons: scored by **Evaluation Rubric** on Competition Website
- No leaderboards for Hackathon Competitions
- Tie: earlier Submission wins
- Top Submissions each get unique ranking, no tiebreakers

### 3.9 PRIZES
- Subject to verification of eligibility and compliance
- Team Prize money split evenly unless Team unanimously opts for different split
- Not eligible if you don't meet eligibility requirements
- Prize acceptance documents due within 2 weeks of notification
- Prizes awarded ~30 days after documents received

### 3.10 TAXES
- All taxes are sole responsibility of winners
- U.S. residents receive IRS Form-1099
